## Etymology
*Cognates with* [[Yēka]] (ear) ([[Sōmyamu Mīsāḍinu (Language)|S. Mīsāḍinu]])
### Noun
1. Eye
2. Seer (religious)
3. Overseer (administrative)

#### Note
In titles, such as "Overseer *of*" or "Manager *of*", the genertive form of Yāka -- *Yākaku* -- is often contracted to Yaggu.

Eg, Overseer of Elections: *[[Caluppagamārrēḍi]] Yāggu*
