## Etymology
From [[Catāse]] in [[Pāta Mīsāḍinu]]. Long ē cannot hold on for long at the end, which is why its not a common end morpheme for established words.

It's a common occurrence for /e:/ to become shortened to /e/. Because Prankithanians find it difficult to end a word on a short e, they often add /yi/ to the end.

### Noun
1. Court of law
2. Royal court
3. Throne/throne room (archaic)
